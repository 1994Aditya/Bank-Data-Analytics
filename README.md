Objective:
Analyze bank loan data to identify trends, patterns, and insights that can inform lending decisions, risk management, and customer segmentation.

Tool used:
1. MS Excel
2. My SQL
3. Power BI
4. Tableau

Business Need:
1. Monitor lending performance: Track key financial KPIs and loan distribution.
2. Manage risk: Identify high-risk loan segments and develop mitigation strategies.
3. Optimize customer targeting: Develop targeted marketing campaigns and loan products.

Insights:
1. Loan distribution: Visualization of loans by product, age group, region, and branch.
2. Risk metrics: Identification of high-risk loan segments and potential losses.
3. Customer demographics: Understanding borrower demographics and behavior.

Recommendations:
1. Diversify loan portfolio: Balance loan distribution across products and segments.
2. Enhance risk management: Develop targeted risk mitigation strategies for high-risk segments.
3. Tailor marketing efforts: Develop targeted marketing campaigns for high-value customer segments.

Conclusion:
The bank loan dashboard provides actionable insights to optimize lending performance, manage risk, and drive business growth. By leveraging these insights, the bank can make informed decisions, improve portfolio management, and enhance customer targeting.
